## 1. Authorized Contributors and Contributions

**Authorized Contributor** means an individual employee or contractor whom Corporation
identifies in an accepted schedule and authorizes to submit material that Corporation
owns or has sufficient authority to license.

**Contribution** means source code, object code, documentation, tests, designs, media,
configuration, or other original material intentionally submitted by an Authorized
Contributor for inclusion in, or development of, Summyz Community.

This Agreement applies only to Contributions that Corporation owns or controls. Every
Authorized Contributor must also accept the then-current Summyz Individual Contributor
License Agreement. Corporation must keep the schedule of Authorized Contributors
accurate and promptly report additions, removals, or changes through the private
procedure designated by the Project Owner.
